# Problem statement

**App name:** GymBuddy
**Meaning:** "Buddy" is gym slang for a training partner. The app is your workout log and your partner finder in one.

**Problem (one sentence):**
Gym-goers forget what they trained and struggle to find training partners, so progress stalls and motivation drops.

| | |
|---|---|
| Who | People who train at a gym regularly or want to start |
| Problem | Workouts are not tracked anywhere useful; finding a partner depends on chance |
| Why it matters | No visible progress and no accountability make people quit |
| Our answer | A cloud workout log plus a place to create and join training sessions |
